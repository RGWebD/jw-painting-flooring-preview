Who we are

JW Painting & Pressure Washing & Epoxy Flooring works from Jacksonville’s 32254 ZIP on the city’s west side. The business describes itself in one line: high-quality painting, expert pressure washing and durable epoxy flooring for homes and businesses, with clean, long-lasting results. This website focuses on the flooring and coating side of that work. Our motto is “Where Precision Meets Perfection.” A customer who hired us to replace the tile in a master bathroom quoted it back to us in a Google review, which tells you it gets said on the job, not just printed on a card.

Meet Jimmie Wilson, owner

Jimmie Wilson runs the business and is on the jobs himself; that is why so many reviews mention him by name. He brings more than 30 years of hands-on experience in painting and finishing work, which is how one crew can handle prep, coatings and the trim work around a new floor. Customers describe the same habits again and again: he answers calls and texts, shows up when he says he will, points out problems worth fixing, and leaves the house clean. One wrote that he sent regular text and photo updates while the job was underway. Another, an HOA homeowner, wrote that he was on site promptly after a pressure-washing notice arrived.

The new floor is rarely the hold-up. What sits under the old one is. Jacksonville slab homes push moisture up through the concrete, and a reading over the product’s limit means adding a barrier or switching material before anything is laid. Leveling and tile both carry cure times. One self-leveler data sheet lists the pour as walkable in 2–3 hours, but moisture-sensitive floor coverings wait 16–24 hours. Standard tile mortar needs a day or more before grout, while some rapid-set mortars allow grout in under three hours and light traffic at four.

Then there is the order of trades. One rigid-core plank guide says to install after other trades finish any work that could damage the floor. These are the findings that most often push a date: Old 9-inch vinyl tile or black adhesive under carpet, which the EPA says to test for asbestos before removal Hollow tile or dips that call for grinding and self-leveler Painting, drywall or cabinet work still planned after the floor

Working backward from closing day, step by step

Speed comes from planning, not from skipping steps. Each step below has a place on the calendar before the crew ever carries in a box.

Start with the date

Tell us the closing, lease or report date and what else is booked around it: movers, cleaners, an inspection or a final walkthrough. The floor schedule is built to finish ahead of those, not on the same morning.

Measure and test the slab

We measure each room and check flatness with a straightedge. On concrete, moisture is tested by the method the flooring maker names, often in-situ humidity probes under ASTM F2170, so the product choice is settled early.

Choose a floor that fits the window

You pick from products that can arrive in time. Many plank and laminate makers ask for 48 hours of acclimation in the room, with the air conditioning running, so delivery is set for at least two days before install.

Prepare the subfloor

Old flooring comes up and adhesive is scraped. A diamond grinder takes down high spots and self-leveler fills low ones. On concrete, the 6-mil poly or underlayment barrier the maker requires goes down on a clean, flat slab.

Install room by room

Floating floors keep their expansion gap at every wall and a transition where the maker limits run length. We work in the order you agree, so movers can fill the finished bedrooms while the crew moves on.

Trim and walk through

Baseboard or quarter-round goes back, thresholds are set, and we walk the floor with you or your agent. You get the product name and color for the closing file or the next owner. On concrete, the barrier the plank maker requires goes down first.

What moves the price when time is short

Square footage and layout set the base. An open great room installs faster than a hallway with six doorways, closets and a staircase. Product availability matters more on a deadline than on any other job. A plank in local stock can be on site this week, while a special-order color may not fit the window at all. Prep found at tear-out, such as a damp slab, a low spot or old tile that needs testing, adds its own line.

An empty house goes faster than a furnished one. Removal and disposal, stairs, new baseboard and moving appliances are each priced separately, and so is any painting you want done before the floor. Your written quote lists the scope and the install days, so you can see whether the date holds before you approve it.

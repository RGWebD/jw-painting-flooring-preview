Epoxy spotlight

Epoxy that bonds to the slab

Most coating failures share one cause: the concrete was never opened up. We start every garage floor by grinding the slab with a diamond grinder, then patching cracks and pits—and we check moisture first.

  • Diamond grinding to the profile the coating maker specifies
  • Moisture testing before any resin is mixed
  • Flake, metallic, solid-color and quartz systems
  • UV-stable clear topcoat where sunlight reaches the floor

Homes and local risks

Wildlight slabs, SR-200 subdivisions and older rural homes

The community is named for David Levy Yulee, who built the Florida Railroad and served as a U.S. senator from Florida. Its population grew from 11,491 in 2010 to 14,195 in 2020. Census survey data puts the median home at 2004, with about four in ten built in 2010 or later. The other side of the housing mix matters just as much. About one in five homes in Yulee is a mobile or manufactured home, a far higher share than in Nocatee or Fruit Cove, according to the same survey. HUD’s federal construction rules apply to manufactured homes that entered production on or after June 15, 1976. Units built before that date were not built to those federal rules, so we check the framing and decking under any floor before new material goes over it.

In the older rural houses and manufactured homes, the work is mostly subfloor repair: soft spots near tubs, sinks, washers and doors, where leaks reach the decking. In the newer subdivisions it is the opposite: sound, young slabs that still carry builder carpet. Water is the shared risk. After Irma in 2017, a News4Jax meteorologist said the Nassau River was among the waterways that flooded in a way not seen in a long time. Soft spots by tubs and washers usually mean the decking under the floor has failed.

From the A1A interchange at I-95 out to the Nassau River

Wildlight sits near the interchange of A1A, which is also SR 200, and I-95. Its first phase covers about 260 acres with 1,000 homes planned. Rayonier built its 55,000-square-foot headquarters there in 2017, and Wildlight Elementary opened that same school year. FDOT’s work to widen SR 200 from four lanes to six runs through the same corridor. Typical jobs are plank in place of builder carpet, flake coatings in new garages, and subfloor repair in older homes on Yulee’s rural roads. Yulee calls fall into two groups: upgrades on new slabs and repairs in older frame and manufactured homes. Here is what each service covers locally.

Buckled & Cupped Floor Repair

Soft or bouncing floors in older manufactured and rural homes usually mean wet or failing decking. We open the area, check the joists and replace the damaged section before the new floor goes down.

Water-Damaged Floor Replacement

A slow leak under a tub or washer can soften decking for years before anyone notices. We tear out to sound material, dry the space and rebuild with moisture-resistant panels and flooring.

Luxury Vinyl Plank Flooring

Plank works in Wildlight slab homes and in manufactured homes alike, once the base is flat and firm. On slabs we add the moisture barrier the maker calls for.

Garage Floor Epoxy & Polyaspartic Coating

New SR-200 garages get a diamond-ground slab and a full-build flake coat that stands up to hot-tire pickup and road grime, usually finished in one to two days.

Rental Property Flooring

Rental homes and manufactured units near I-95 need floors that survive turnover. Durable plank over a repaired base cuts repeat work, and multi-unit pricing applies to several units at once.

Tile Flooring Installation

Owners of new homes often swap small builder ceramic in baths for porcelain. In frame homes, we stiffen the subfloor and use an uncoupling membrane so the tile stays bonded.

Flooring Installation

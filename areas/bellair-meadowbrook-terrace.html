Blanding Boulevard ranches built on 1960s and 1970s slabs

Most streets between the Ortega River and Kingsley Avenue filled in early. The Census counted 12,144 residents here in 1980 and 14,482 in 2020, so Bellair, Meadowbrook, Grove Park and Loch Rane are mostly the houses first built there. Many still rest on their original concrete slabs. Those slabs carry layers of history. Vinyl composition tile, sheet vinyl and the black mastic under them turn up often when old carpet comes out. The U.S. EPA says older vinyl floor tiles, sheet-vinyl backing and some adhesives may contain asbestos.

So the crew treats original tile and dark adhesive as suspect. Nothing gets ground or scraped until a sample has come back, and a well-bonded layer can often stay put under a floating floor. Age shows in the concrete too. Slabs this old settle, crack and dip, and a rigid plank or large porcelain tile will rock over a hump or a hollow. The fix is grinding the highs, filling the lows with cement-based self-leveler and checking the plane with a long straightedge. That prep follows ASTM F710, the standard for readying concrete to receive resilient flooring. A moisture reading comes first, since a glue-down floor on a damp slab fails from underneath.

Typical jobs are whole-house plank replacing carpet and VCT, kitchen and bath tile, and quick turnovers in the duplexes and apartments off Blanding.

Each card ties a service to what we usually find under the carpet in these 1960s and 1970s houses.

Flooring Installation

Whole-house replacements here start with removing old layers, a slab moisture reading and leveling. Then plank, porcelain or laminate goes down flat, without the humps old concrete hides under carpet.

Luxury Vinyl Plank Flooring

Floating plank can often go over tight, flat VCT, which leaves suspect tile undisturbed as the EPA suggests. A polyethylene barrier goes under it when the slab test or the product requires one.

Tile Flooring Installation

Porcelain suits the kitchens and baths in these ranches, but only on a flat slab. Old shrinkage cracks get an uncoupling membrane so they do not telegraph through the new grout lines.

Popped & Cracked Tile Repair

Decades-old ceramic set without perimeter movement joints tends to pop and crack as these slabs heat and cool. We lift the loose section, add the missing joint and match the repair closely.

Rental Property Flooring

Renters outnumber owners in this CDP, so turnover work is steady. Durable plank, tight scheduling and multi-unit pricing help landlords get units off Blanding back on the market sooner.

Water-Damaged Floor Replacement

Original supply lines and water heaters in houses this age do fail. We pull the wet flooring, find how far water spread across the slab and let it dry before anything new goes in.

Garage Floor Epoxy & Polyaspartic Coating

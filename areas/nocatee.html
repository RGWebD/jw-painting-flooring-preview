Young slabs and builder carpet off Nocatee Parkway

St. Johns County approved Nocatee in 2001 as a Development of Regional Impact. The PARC Group began selling homes in 2007, the same year it finished Nocatee Parkway, the 4.83-mile road between US 1 and the Ponte Vedra side. Growth since then has been steep. The census counted 4,524 residents in 2010 and 22,503 in 2020. RCLCO, which ranks master-planned communities by annual sales, placed it fifth in the country in 2019. Its 2025 list still included Nocatee, at No. 40 with 417 sales. Census survey data puts the median home here at 2015, and more than eight in ten were built in 2010 or later.

For floors, that age is the whole story. Most of these houses sit on slabs poured in the last decade or so, and new concrete gives up its moisture slowly. A builder slab is also troweled smooth, which is fine under carpet pad but too slick for a coating to grip. So the first visit is a moisture test and a close look at the surface, not a sales pitch. Then we can say whether glue-down plank, floating plank or tile makes sense, and what the garage needs before it is coated. Most homes here sit on slabs poured in the last decade or so.

These are the first-year upgrades we are asked about most, with the reason each one fits a new home here.

Luxury Vinyl Plank Flooring

Plank is the usual replacement for builder carpet. On a young slab we check the moisture reading first and lay the barrier the plank maker calls for, so the floor stays flat through humid summers.

Garage Floor Epoxy & Polyaspartic Coating

New garage slabs are smooth and still curing. We diamond-grind for grip, confirm moisture, and use a full-build system that resists hot-tire pickup, typically finished in one to two days.

Tile Flooring Installation

Owners often trade small builder tile in baths and laundry rooms for large-format porcelain. We flatten the slab, add perimeter movement joints and set tile with full mortar coverage.

Flooring Installation

Redoing the whole first floor at once, carpet and tile together, saves a second round of disruption. One measure, one moisture test and one written quote cover every room, and one crew handles the sequence.

Hardwood & Laminate Flooring

Owners who want real wood on a new slab do better with engineered boards. They move less in humid air than solid wood and can go down once the slab tests dry enough.

Pool Deck, Patio & Lanai Coatings

Lanais and new pool decks pick up mildew quickly in humid summers. We pressure-wash the concrete, then add a textured coating that is easier to keep clean underfoot.

Fast Move-In Flooring Installation

Some buyers want floors in before the moving truck arrives. If the slab tests dry and materials are in stock, the main rooms can be finished between closing and move-in. Reviews from homeowners across St. Johns County

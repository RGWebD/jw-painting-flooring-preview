Resort-era homes and oceanfront lots on San Pablo Island

The town began as Mineral City, a mining camp that grew after titanium-bearing minerals were found in the dunes in 1914. The National Lead Company bought the mining operations in 1921 and later turned the land into a resort, borrowing the name of Pontevedra, Spain, from an old map. The Ponte Vedra Inn and Club opened in 1928. That history left a wide range of homes, from resort-era houses to oceanfront estates and newer gated neighborhoods. Owners often choose travertine, marble, large porcelain and wide-plank wood, and those materials punish shortcuts. Stone and big tile need a flat, sound slab, and TCNA Handbook EJ171 tightens field movement joints to 8 to 12 feet where sun or moisture reach the floor.

Floor Sealing & Clear Coat Protection

Existing marble, travertine and polished concrete can often be cleaned and sealed rather than replaced. The right sealer slows staining from sand, salt and spills without making the surface slick.

Hardwood & Laminate Flooring

Wide-plank engineered wood moves less than solid boards in salty, humid air. We test the slab and the wood, acclimate the boards and choose a glue-down or floating method to suit the reading.

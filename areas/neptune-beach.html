Pull back the old carpet in a postwar Neptune Beach house and you may find terrazzo underneath. The town sits about 16 miles east of our west-side base, between Atlantic Beach and Jacksonville Beach. Neptune split from Jacksonville Beach in 1931 after a tax revolt and has stayed small ever since. It is the smallest of the beach cities, with about 2.3 square miles of land and 7,217 residents at the 2020 census. That small footprint filled in early. Census Bureau estimates for 2020 to 2024 date about 30 percent of homes to the 1950s and 1960s, and roughly 64 percent to before 1980. Nearly seven in ten are detached houses, and the survey counts no buildings of 20 or more units. In other words, this is mostly a town of houses on modest lots, not condo towers.

Many of those 1950s and 1960s houses were finished with terrazzo, a mix of marble chips set in cement and divided by thin metal strips. Later owners often covered it with wall-to-wall carpet, and tack strip holes, glue and paint spatter are common once it comes back up. Sound terrazzo can be ground and polished again. Cracked or patched terrazzo may be better covered. Either way, it is worth deciding before you order anything, because terrazzo changes what goes on top and how much prep it needs. Terrazzo often survives intact under decades of carpet.

Beaches Town Center sits where Atlantic Boulevard meets the ocean, and the boulevard is the line between Neptune Beach to the south and Atlantic Beach to the north. Most Neptune Beach projects start with one question: keep the terrazzo, or cover it. These services cover both answers.

Floor Sealing & Clear Coat Protection

Once terrazzo is cleaned, ground and polished, a penetrating sealer or clear coat protects it from beach sand and spills. Resealing later is far easier than a second restoration.

Luxury Vinyl Plank Flooring

Floating plank goes over sound terrazzo with little prep and keeps the original floor intact underneath. A moisture barrier on the slab is added when the reading calls for one.

Tile Flooring Installation

Porcelain over terrazzo needs a roughened or primed surface for the mortar to grip. Over cracks we use an uncoupling membrane so old breaks do not telegraph through.

Flooring Installation

Small ranch houses often make sense to redo all at once, so one material flows through every room. We plan the order of rooms so furniture has somewhere to go.

Popped & Cracked Tile Repair

Bath tile in houses from the 1950s and 1960s can have loose or cracked pieces. We replace the damaged section and add the perimeter movement joints that TCNA Handbook EJ171 calls for.

Water-Damaged Floor Replacement

Slab homes this close to the ocean see storm water and the odd plumbing leak. Terrazzo itself survives water well, but carpet, pad and plank above it usually come out.

Garage Floor Epoxy & Polyaspartic Coating

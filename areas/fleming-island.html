Flooring Contractor in Fleming Island, FL

Fleming Island homes went up mostly in the 1990s and 2000s, and many are due for their first full floor update. The island sits about 19 miles from our Jacksonville base, off US-17 between Doctors Lake and Black Creek. Calls usually involve engineered wood, plank, tile and screened lanai coatings. Homes and local risks

From the old Navy airfield to Eagle Harbor fairways

The name goes back to George Fleming, an Irish immigrant who founded the Hibernia plantation here in 1790 under a Spanish land grant. The suburb itself is far younger. In 1942 the Navy built a four-runway auxiliary field for NAS Jacksonville, abandoned it in the 1960s, and the runways were torn out in the late 1990s for housing. Building moved fast after that. The housing count climbed from about 2,400 units in 1990 to 6,130 in 2000 and 10,440 in 2010. In 1998 a 2,129-acre tract won approval for Fleming Island Plantation, while Eagle Harbor grew around a semi-private Clyde Johnston golf course on Eagle Harbor Parkway.

These are larger homes, many backing onto fairways, ponds or open water. Builder carpet is giving way to engineered hardwood and plank. NWFA guidelines call for checking the moisture of both the wood and the concrete before install, and engineered boards move less with humidity than solid wood. That matters on an island bordered by a river, a lake and a creek. Screened lanais are the other standard feature. Their slabs take sun, rain and pool water, so a decorative coating needs a pressure-washed, profiled surface and a texture that stays grippy underfoot when wet.

Slab and boards are both moisture-checked before install. Local knowledge

Working along US-17, CR 220 and the Black Creek shoreline

US-17 runs the length of the island, and County Road 220 meets it. A Modern Cities photo comparison shows the land around that intersection still largely undeveloped in 1970. Fleming Island High School opened in 2003, and the 32003 ZIP code followed in 2004. Water frames every address: the St. Johns River to the east, Doctors Lake to the north, Black Creek to the south and Swimming Pen Creek to the west. During Irma in 2017, Black Creek crested at a record of about 28.5 feet, per Clay County emergency managers, and docks out to Doctors Lake were badly damaged.

Typical jobs are engineered wood or plank through the main living areas, porcelain in baths and entries, and coatings on lanais and pool decks. Fleming Island Plantation’s community district alone covers about 1,580 acres, and villages with their own HOAs may set work hours and contractor check-in rules. These are the projects owners on the island ask about most, matched to the way homes here are built and used.

Hardwood & Laminate Flooring

Engineered hardwood is the usual step up from builder carpet in the larger homes. It moves less than solid wood in humid air, and it goes down only after the slab and boards are moisture-tested.

Luxury Vinyl Plank Flooring

Plank suits kitchens, family rooms and pool baths where wet feet come in from the lanai. A vapor barrier goes beneath it on the slab whenever the moisture test or the plank maker requires one.

Pool Deck, Patio & Lanai Coatings

Screened lanais and pool decks get decorative coatings over a pressure-washed, repaired and profiled slab. The finish carries a slip-resistant texture, which matters most where kids run from the pool.

Tile Flooring Installation

Large porcelain in baths and entries needs a flat slab and movement joints at the walls. Rooms with big river-facing sliders get tighter joint spacing where the afternoon sun heats the tile.

Buckled & Cupped Floor Repair

Laminate and solid wood installed in the 2000s sometimes cup or peak near sliders and during humid summers. We trace the moisture source first, then repair or replace the boards that moved.

Water-Damaged Floor Replacement

Homes near Black Creek, Doctors Lake and the river face storm water, and any house can lose a supply line. We pull wet flooring, dry and test the slab, then rebuild with suitable material.

Garage Floor Epoxy & Polyaspartic Coating

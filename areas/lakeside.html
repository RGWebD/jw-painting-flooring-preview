Doctors Lake subdivisions from the 1980s building boom

This part of Clay County grew in a hurry. The Census counted 10,534 residents in 1980 and 29,137 in 1990, nearly three times as many, and the figure has stayed near 31,000 since. A large share of the houses went up in one decade, and they are aging on the same schedule. Builders of that era finished living areas with wall-to-wall carpet and wet rooms with small ceramic tile set on the slab. Thirty-odd years on, the carpet is matted and stained, and the tile shows cracked grout or hollow spots. Whole-house luxury vinyl plank is the swap we see most, because it handles spills and humidity far better than carpet.

Those slabs need checking before anything new goes on them. We use in-situ relative-humidity probes under ASTM F2170, or a calcium chloride test under ASTM F1869, and add a barrier when the numbers call for it. Original tile that tented usually lacked the perimeter movement joints TCNA Handbook EJ171 calls for at walls. Doctors Lake adds its own risk. The lake covers about 13 square miles and is brackish, fed by the St. Johns River through Doctors Inlet. During Irma in 2017, Clay County Emergency Management reported roughly 1,200 flooded homes countywide, and docks from Middleburg to Doctors Lake were wrecked.

Kingsley Avenue (State Road 224) forms the northern edge, with Bellair-Meadowbrook Terrace across the road and Orange Park to the northeast. To the southeast, US-17 crosses Doctors Inlet toward Fleming Island. Local history turns up in odd places. Yale scientist Robert Yerkes opened a primate research laboratory here in 1930, and the old site is now part of the Foxwood Center plaza.

These are the requests that come up most in 1980s and 1990s houses here, with the reason each one fits.

Luxury Vinyl Plank Flooring

The single most common swap. Plank replaces worn builder carpet room by room, sits on a moisture-tested slab with a vapor barrier where needed, and shrugs off the spills that ruined the old carpet.

Tile Flooring Installation

Open plans from the 1990s look best with one porcelain running from entry to kitchen. Long runs get field movement joints laid out on grout lines, so the pattern stays clean and the tile stays bonded.

Popped & Cracked Tile Repair

Original ceramic in these homes often cracks or tents where nobody left a gap at the walls. We replace the failed section and restore the movement joint that should have been there all along.

Garage Floor Epoxy & Polyaspartic Coating

Garages from this era are usually bare, oil-stained concrete. Diamond grinding gives the coating something to grip, and a broadcast flake system hides old marks and hairline cracks in one or two days.

Water-Damaged Floor Replacement

Houses near the lake and its creeks face storm water, and thirty-year-old supply lines do give out. We remove wet flooring, dry and check the slab, then rebuild with water-tolerant material.

Buckled & Cupped Floor Repair

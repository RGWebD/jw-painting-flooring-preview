Builder-grade floors in the newest CR-210 subdivisions

Mail here was addressed to Jacksonville until 2005, when a chamber of commerce poll chose the name "St. Johns" over Fruit Cove, River Oaks and Bartram. Growth since has been fast. The county grew from 190,039 people in 2010 to 273,425 in 2020, about 44 percent. Census survey data for the 32259 ZIP puts the median home at 2009. Nearly half were built in 2010 or later, and about 3,700 went up in 2020 or after. Many came with a basic builder floor package: carpet in bedrooms and living areas, small ceramic in wet rooms, and a bare garage slab.

Replacing that package soon after closing is simple in principle, but young concrete holds water longer than people expect. A slab poured a year or two ago can still read too wet for glue-down plank or a coating. We test every slab before choosing materials, and we plan the order of work so the garage, the tile rooms and the plank rooms do not trip over each other. The builder’s warranty terms are worth reading before anything changes, too. The standard builder package: carpet in living areas, small tile in wet rooms.

In April 2026 the county finished widening CR 210 between I-95 and US 1 from two lanes to six, along the stretch from Trinity Way to Beachwalk Boulevard. The developer of the Twin Creeks neighborhoods, including Beachwalk and Beacon Lake, was to fund that work. Beachside High opened east of I-95 for the 2022–23 school year. On the west side, the county is rebuilding Race Track Road into a six-lane urban section from West Peyton Parkway toward Bartram Park Boulevard. Down toward the river, RiverTown sits between Longleaf Pine Parkway and SR 13; RCLCO ranked it 27th nationally in 2025 sales.

Typical jobs are whole-house plank swaps, porcelain in primary baths and flake coatings in two- and three-car garages. These are the upgrades new owners here ask for most, and why each one suits a recently built home.

Luxury Vinyl Plank Flooring

Plank is the usual swap for builder carpet in living areas and bedrooms. We check the young slab, lay the maker’s moisture barrier and keep the expansion gap the maker’s install guide asks for.

Garage Floor Epoxy & Polyaspartic Coating

Builder garage slabs are smooth and bare. We diamond-grind, patch any shrinkage cracks and apply a full-build flake system that resists hot-tire pickup, typically in one to two days.

Tile Flooring Installation

Small builder ceramic in baths and laundry rooms often gives way to large-format porcelain. We flatten the slab, add perimeter movement joints and back-butter each tile for full coverage.

Flooring Installation

Doing every room in one project means one measure, one moisture test and one written quote. The crew sequences carpet removal, tile and plank so the house is torn up only once.

Fast Move-In Flooring Installation

Buyers moving in on a tight closing date can get the main rooms done before furniture arrives, provided the slab reading allows it and materials are in stock.

Epoxy Flooring

Some owners want a coated floor beyond the garage, in a workshop, gym or bonus room on the slab. We grind and test the concrete first, then pick a flake, solid or metallic finish.

Rental Property Flooring

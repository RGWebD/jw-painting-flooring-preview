Flooring Contractor in Oakleaf Plantation, FL

Most homes in Oakleaf Plantation went up after 2000, so the calls here are about upgrades more than repairs. The community sits about 17 miles from our Jacksonville base, along the First Coast Expressway. Owners usually want flake epoxy in two- and three-car garages and plank in place of builder-grade carpet. Homes and local risks

Master-planned streets on the old Branan Field

The community is a 6,400-acre planned development the Hutson Companies started in 2002. Most of it lies in unincorporated Clay County, with a slice inside Jacksonville city limits. Growth was fast: the CDP counted 20,315 residents in 2010 and 31,034 in 2020. Housing numbers tell the same story. There were about 1,100 homes in the area in 2000 and roughly 9,000 by the 2017–2021 Census estimates, close to nine in ten of them single-family houses. That makes this one of the youngest housing stocks the crew works in, with builder finishes now ten to twenty years old.

The land has a past, though. The Clay County Clerk of Court notes that the Navy’s Branan Field outlying landing field, an octagon of 4,000-foot runways used for early Blue Angels training, was sold in 1953. Its footprint now lies under subdivisions along Branan Field Road. Garages are where these houses differ most from older Clay County stock. Two- and three-car bays are common, and their newer concrete takes a coating well once it is ground to the right surface profile on ICRI’s CSP scale. Hot tires, summer heat and fertilizer spills are what the coating has to survive.

Grinding opens the concrete so the coating can bond. Local knowledge

Between Argyle Forest Boulevard and the First Coast Expressway

Argyle Forest Boulevard runs east from the community to Blanding Boulevard (State Road 21), and Oakleaf Plantation Parkway meets the First Coast Expressway on the west side. Schools anchor the villages, from Oakleaf Village Elementary to Oakleaf Junior High and Oakleaf High School. Roughly four in five occupied homes here are owner-occupied, according to the University of Florida GeoPlan Center’s census profile. So most calls come from owners planning upgrades rather than landlords turning units. Typical jobs are flake epoxy across a full garage, LVP replacing first-floor carpet, and stair and upstairs updates in two-story plans.

If your village has a homeowners association, read its covenants before choosing a garage color or an exterior coating. The rules vary from one section to the next. These are the services owners here ask about most, with the reason each fits a house built in the last twenty-odd years.

Garage Floor Epoxy & Polyaspartic Coating

A three-car garage gives a flake system room to pay off. We grind, patch the control-joint edges, broadcast flake to full coverage and topcoat, so hot tires do not lift the finish in July.

Epoxy Floor Repair & Recoating

Some garages already carry a DIY or builder coating that is peeling along the tire paths. We grind off the failed layer, repair the concrete beneath it and recoat with a higher-build system.

Luxury Vinyl Plank Flooring

Builder carpet on the first floor is the usual target. Plank on a moisture-tested slab handles kids, pets and wet shoes, and it can run straight through into the kitchen without a threshold.

Flooring Installation

Two-story plans often mix materials by level. One project can cover plank downstairs, the upstairs rooms and matching stair treads, so transitions line up and the crew is in and out once.

Tile Flooring Installation

Builder ceramic in baths and laundry rooms is dated in many of these homes. Porcelain over an uncoupling membrane refreshes the look and keeps hairline slab cracks out of the new grout.

Floor Sealing & Clear Coat Protection
